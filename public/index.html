How the numbers are worked out

Cooling required

mass flow = density × thickness × width × speed
heat = mass flow × specific heat × (temp in − temp out)

Water side

heat = water flow × 998 kg/m³ × 4182 J/kg·K × (water out − water in)

Product on a roll

contact time = π × diameter × (wrap ÷ 360) ÷ speed
temp out = roll temp + (temp in − roll temp) × e−U × time ÷ (density × cp × thickness)

Rolls in a zone run one after another: each roll’s temp out is the next roll’s temp in.

Roll effectiveness is how much of the possible cooling the roll achieved: (temp in − temp out) ÷ (temp in − roll temp). 100% means the product left at the roll temperature.

Assumptions

  • The product is thin enough that its temperature is even through its thickness. For thick or plastic products the inside lags the surface, so predictions are optimistic. Calibrate U to correct for it.
  • U (heat transfer coefficient) covers contact between the product and the roll. Typical values are 100–1500 W/m²·K and depend on contact pressure, surface finish and air entrainment. Calibrate it from a measured temperature for real numbers.
  • Cooling in the air gaps between rolls is not counted.
  • Material properties are typical room-temperature values. Edit them under “Material properties”.
